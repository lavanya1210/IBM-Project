Recipe Name: Classic Chocolate Cake

Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 3/4 cup cocoa powder
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking powder

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
2. Mix all dry ingredients.
3. Add eggs, milk, butter, and vanilla.
4. Pour batter into a greased cake pan.
5. Bake for 35 minutes.

Nutrition (Approximate):
Calories: 320 kcal
Protein: 6 g
Carbohydrates: 45 g
Fat: 14 g

Substitutions:
Eggs → Yogurt (1/4 cup per egg)
Butter → Coconut Oil
Sugar → Stevia

Suitable Diet:
Vegetarian
